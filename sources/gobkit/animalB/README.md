# Goat asset intake — visually rejected

**2026-11-02 status: withdrawn from runtime loading and the scene inspector.** The round body, oversized eyes, tiny legs and thick horns do not meet the distant landmark animal direction. Recoloring this model does not make it an acceptable mountain-goat silhouette. Keep the files for provenance and comparison, not as a visually approved deliverable. See [the withdrawal scope](../../../docs/scopes/wildlife-goat-withdrawal.md). Rebuilding the derivative must not remove its `enabled: false` gate in `src/content/assets.ts`.

Original intake (HOW-WILDLIFE-PHASE2): Gobkit *Free Animal Pack Vol. 2*, https://gobkit.com/freebies and https://gobkit.com/freebies/animalB/Goat.glb. The official no-login catalogue https://gobkit.com/api/free lists Goat as CC0-1.0. Original SHA-256: `51f7399bb76ea5175ac4d7c136c50b74f86250664c1f6b1acff48e5322f09ea0`. 16-bone rig, 396 triangles. Source metadata remains in `catalog.json`; the CC0 text remains in `public/assets/licenses/gobkit-CC0-1.0.txt`.

The earlier derivative recolored the embedded atlas to off-white/dark hooves and retained idle/walk clips. These are historical modifications, not a successful art adaptation. The accepted stag is unchanged. No replacement goat model has been admitted.
